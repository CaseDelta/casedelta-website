## The five kinds of AI a PI firm can buy

1. **Demand letter and records tools.** Built around the documents a PI case produces: demands, medical chronologies and record summaries.
2. **Plaintiff AI platforms.** One product meant to run much of the case, from intake to settlement.
3. **AI inside your case system.** The assistant your case management vendor builds into its own product.
4. **General AI chatbots.** Assistants for writing and research across a whole company.
5. **Cross-system AI paralegals.** An assistant that works inside every system the firm already uses, at once.

## How to choose

- **You send a high volume of demands.** Start with a demand letter tool.
- **You want one product to become your case system.** Look at a plaintiff AI platform.
- **You run on one case system and want AI inside it.** Start with that vendor's own AI.
- **You need writing and research help, not case work.** A general chatbot is enough.
- **Your case, intake, email and documents live in different systems.** A cross-system AI paralegal is the category built for that.

Many firms use more than one. A demand tool and a cross-system assistant do different jobs and can run side by side.

## Questions firms ask

### What is the best AI for a personal injury law firm?

It depends on the job. Demand letter tools are strongest on demands and chronologies, case-system AI is strongest inside its own product, and a cross-system AI paralegal is strongest when the work spans several systems.

### Is ChatGPT safe for client files?

That depends on the plan and the firm's own review. ABA Formal Opinion 512 (July 2024) asks lawyers to understand whether an AI tool uses client information to train itself before they put client data into it.

### Do these tools replace a paralegal?

No. Each one drafts, finds and organizes. A lawyer or paralegal still reviews the work and makes the decisions.
